PrescribePro Rx

PrescribePro Rx is a browser-based clinical workflow application designed to help healthcare professionals manage patient information, create prescriptions, maintain prescription history, and manage application settings.

How it works

Users can sign in with their Google Account. Application information may be stored locally in the browser and, when enabled by the application, backed up to the user's Google Drive application-data storage.

Google Account information is used to authenticate users and associate application data with the appropriate user account.
