Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PrescribePro Rx handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Google, PrescribePro Rx may receive basic Google Account information necessary for authentication, including your name, email address, and basic profile information.

The application may also process information that you enter into the application, such as patient information, prescription information, profile settings, and application configuration data.

2. Browser Storage

PrescribePro Rx uses browser local storage to retain application information on the device being used. Information stored in browser local storage remains on that device until it is removed by the application, the browser, or the user.

Because browser local storage is associated with the browser and device, users should protect access to their device and browser profile.

3. Google Drive Application Data

PrescribePro Rx may use Google's Drive application-data storage to back up application information associated with the signed-in user.

The application requests the Google Drive application-data permission so that it can store and retrieve its own application data. It does not request general access to the user's Google Drive files.

Data stored through this feature is associated with the user's Google Account and is stored using Google's infrastructure.

4. How Google User Data Is Used

Google Account information is used to authenticate the user and associate application information with the correct account.

Google Drive application-data access is used to provide application backup and synchronization functionality.

PrescribePro Rx does not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the operation of the application.

5. Data Sharing

PrescribePro Rx does not intentionally disclose application data to unrelated third parties except where necessary to provide services used by the application, such as Google authentication, Google Drive storage, and analytics functionality.

The application currently uses Google services for authentication, Google Drive application-data storage, and Google Analytics.

6. Google Analytics

The application currently loads Google Analytics to help understand application usage and improve the service. Google Analytics may collect information about interactions with the website and the device or browser used to access it, according to Google's applicable policies and configuration.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application information. However, no browser-based or internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid storing information in the application unless they are satisfied that the device, browser account, Google Account, and surrounding environment are appropriately protected.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally in the browser may remain until it is deleted from the application or browser storage.

Data backed up to Google Drive application-data storage may remain associated with the user's Google Account until it is deleted through the application's available deletion functions or otherwise removed from the associated account.

9. Revoking Google Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their Google Account through their Google Account security and connected-app controls.

10. Children's Privacy

PrescribePro Rx is intended for professional or adult users and is not directed toward children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
